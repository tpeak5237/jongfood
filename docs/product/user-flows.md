# JongFood user flows

## Customer pre-order

1. Customer lands on the active lunch surface and sees open shops plus live wait ranges.
2. Customer picks a shop and scans categories without leaving the page.
3. Customer adds menu items; each line carries a price and capacity load.
4. Customer selects a capacity-aware pickup window with remaining order and work-unit counts.
5. Customer confirms reservation and receives a reference, queue number, slot, total, and estimate.
6. Customer follows the status timeline from confirmed to preparing to ready.

## Customer walk-in

1. Customer chooses “Join walk-in queue.”
2. JongFood assigns a queue number and computes a range from the same preparation load as pre-orders.
3. The order appears in the shared customer tracker and merchant queue.

## Merchant queue

1. Staff switches to Back of house.
2. New / confirmed orders, preparing orders, and ready orders are separated into scan-friendly columns.
3. Staff advances one valid status at a time; the next action is explicit.
4. The wait panel exposes the customer-facing range and current load.

## Manager controls

1. Manager can pause or resume online ordering.
2. Manager can change base units per minute, buffer, and manual delay.
3. Customer wait ranges recalculate from the same settings.
4. Manager can toggle individual menu availability without deleting the menu item.
