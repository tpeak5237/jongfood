# JongFood merchant guide

## Start of service

1. Open **Back of house** and confirm the service toggle says **Taking orders**.
2. Check **Speed settings** for the current capacity per minute and buffer.
3. Review **Menu & availability** and switch off anything sold out.
4. Keep the queue board open on a tablet near the preparation area.

## During the rush

- Move orders through **New / confirmed → Preparing → Ready for pickup**.
- Use the wait range as an operational signal, not a promise to customers.
- Add counter orders with **Add walk-in order** so the walk-in enters the same queue.
- Pause online orders when the kitchen cannot accept more work. Resume only after the queue is safe.

## Walk-in fairness

Walk-in orders do not automatically bypass scheduled pre-orders. The production priority service should use committed ready time, then confirmation time, and record any manual override reason.
